What's behind our line?

Assumptions and why we sometimes pass

These are full-league season simulations using our possession engine. Historical injury/illness absences are sampled and each affected rotation is rebuilt. Per-game averages use games played, not all 82 scheduled games. Shooting percentages use each season’s total makes divided by attempts; seasons with no attempts have no percentage. Percentages are descriptive projections, not betting signals. Attempts per game are shown for context. The schedule contains provisional Cup matchups. Incomplete historical records can understate absences; this is not a current medical or return-date forecast.

The displayed 10th–90th percentile range describes simulated season outcomes, not the range of individual games and not real-world confidence. The comparison also stresses production up and down by 10%. That stress is a research rule, not fitted uncertainty.

We do not turn historical player value or a simulated average into an award probability. Without a matching period, stat, player identity, settlement rule and usable quote, the result is Pass. These models have not established a validated player-prop betting edge.
