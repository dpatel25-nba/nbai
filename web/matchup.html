How we modeled this matchup

Pricing, coverage and model limits

Prices are the observed best asks for one $1-payout contract, before fees. They are not last-trade prices or guaranteed fills. Quotes older than 75 seconds cannot produce favorites. Size is the displayed quantity at that ask; a larger purchase can cost more.

Our favorites rank the lower 95% simulation sampling bound minus the ask and an estimated one-contract taker fee: round up 0.07 × price × (1 − price) to the next cent. This bound measures simulation noise, not model accuracy. Fees can differ by contract and execution. We show a maximum of three distinct players with a positive margin; profit is not established.

All probabilities assume participation and normal game completion. Player nonparticipation and game cancellation can lead to fair-price settlement under Kalshi’s rules, which is not modeled here. Protected, head-to-head, special and period markets are outside this comparison.
