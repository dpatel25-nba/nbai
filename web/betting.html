What we tested—and what we passed on

200 full-league seasons, 246,000 simulated games, all 30 teams. Both sides of all 312 collected win-total contracts were screened. The team cases below are editorial research selections after inspecting that screen, not untouched predictions.

Every player is available in the scenario, rotations stay fixed, and 30 games use provisional Cup-slot opponents. Five-win stress means moving each simulated win total five wins against the selection; it is not a fitted injury model. No calibrated award model or playoff simulation is available.

The large Indiana under signal is held for review of returning-player recovery and team calibration. Championship picks are deferred because this engine does not simulate the playoffs. Related win thresholds are overlapping outcomes.
