How we choose picks & read prices

Each featured line has a research review covering current news, availability, role, historical data and a counterargument. The simulator is a supporting check while it is being developed; its forecasts are not displayed here. We have not established a reliable betting edge.

Each matchup shows three distinct players ranked using recent minutes, stat opportunities, historical threshold results and observed prices. These are relative favorites, not calibrated win probabilities or proof of positive expected return. Known unavailable and uncertain players are excluded. Source gaps and historical context are retained in Research & sources.

Prices are observed asks per $1-payout contract before fees, not guaranteed fills. Quotes older than 75 seconds are marked saved. The comparison estimates the general quadratic taker fee, rounded up for one contract; actual fees depend on contract and execution. Prices can change after the picks are published.

Game-script combos are proposed combinations, with no verified acceptance, joint price or payout. A coherent story does not establish parlay value. Nonparticipation or cancellation may result in fair-price settlement under the contract rules.
