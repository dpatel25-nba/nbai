How we choose picks & read prices

Each featured line has a research review covering current news, availability, role, historical data and a counterargument. The simulator is a supporting check while it is being developed; its forecasts are not displayed here. We have not established a reliable betting edge.

“Within our price limit” requires an unexpired review, a usable availability snapshot, model support after the estimated fee, and a current offer at or below the reviewed ceiling. A maximum of three distinct players is shown. Reviewed ideas can remain visible with an explicit wait or closed label; unreviewed lines are never promoted to fill a slot.

Prices are observed asks per $1-payout contract before fees, not guaranteed fills. Quotes older than 75 seconds are marked saved. The comparison estimates the general quadratic taker fee, rounded up for one contract; actual fees depend on contract and execution. Price ceilings are analyst judgments.

Game-script combos are proposed combinations, with no verified acceptance, joint price or payout. A coherent story does not establish parlay value. Nonparticipation or cancellation may result in fair-price settlement under the contract rules.
