WHY NBAI EXISTS

More basketball.
Better questions.

NBAI is a place to follow an idea a little further. We bring original statistical research and interactive simulations together so you can explore the league from your own point of view.

We’ll show our work. Simulations are possible outcomes, not promises. Our market watchlist is being researched, and we haven’t established a reliable betting edge. You’ll find the assumptions and limitations alongside the analysis.
