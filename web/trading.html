Where these numbers come from

Archive coverage is loading.

Selections use the original fixed model-surplus threshold of 5¢ after its 1¢ selection allowance and fee reserve. Your price allowance changes hypothetical execution costs, not which side the original model selected. The fee scenario uses the archived quadratic fee assumption with cent rounding; historical applicability is unverified.

Each replay day reserves all selected costs before crediting that day's eventual settlements. This daily accounting is a sandbox assumption, not actual exchange settlement timing. Available size, real fills and contemporaneous source availability are unverified. Returns are hypothetical, and this previously examined period is not fresh validation. Trying multiple settings here does not establish an edge.
