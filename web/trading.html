Every price has a boundary.

The patient limit is your draft entry. The model ceiling is a boundary, not a reason to chase a rising price.

Where these limits and sizes come from

1. A model ceiling

Each pick uses 2,000 joint game simulations. The ceiling is the simulation sampling lower bound minus a 5¢ research margin and a 2¢ fee reserve, rounded down to a whole cent. Sampling bounds do not measure forecast error. These probabilities have not established a profitable strategy.

2. A patient limit

We take the lower of that ceiling and the saved reference ask minus your selected discount, then respect the contract's price grid. The 2¢ default is an experimental timing rule—not an estimate of the day's lowest price. Refreshing quotes never moves this reference.

3. Fee-aware sizing

The example fee is round up to the next cent of 0.07 × contracts × price × (1 − price). We fit principal plus this fee inside your cap. Actual maker fees, contract exceptions and fill rounding may differ; confirm the displayed total in Kalshi. A resting order can fill partially or never fill.

4. An expiration

Review or cancel by the earlier of forecast expiry and five minutes before tipoff. New information can invalidate the pick earlier. The website cannot cancel an order you enter. Stale quotes, changed contract rules and expired forecasts withhold entry instructions.
